Why AuditoriaX

Everything you need to
book, manage & attend

From real-time seat maps to instant digital tickets — AuditoriaX makes campus event booking effortless for students, teachers, and organizers alike.

💺

Real-time Seat Selection

Watch seats update live as students across your campus book them. No more double-bookings, no more confusion. Our real-time engine powered by Socket.IO ensures every seat state is perfectly synced.

⚡ Powered by Socket.IO
🏛️

Multi-College Network

Browse and book events from 50+ colleges in one place. Students can attend events beyond their campus walls, connecting communities like never before.

🌐 Cross-campus Access
🎟️

Instant Digital Tickets

Confirm your booking and get a beautiful digital ticket with a unique barcode instantly. Show it at the door — no printing needed.

✨ QR + Barcode Powered
🧭

Smart Seat Allocation

Auditoriums are intelligently divided into teacher, girls, and boys sections with cluster-based access control for fair distribution.

🔑 Cluster-based Access
📊

Powerful Analytics

Admins get deep insights — booking trends, venue occupancy, category breakdowns, and revenue charts — all in a live dashboard.

📈 Live Reports
💳

Seamless Payments

Support for UPI, cards, and net banking. Free events stay free, paid ones process securely with 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI DSS compliance.

🔒 PCI DSS Compliant
