Features

Everything your college needs,
nothing it doesn't.

From the first signup to the last QR scan, AuditoriaX handles every step of event management.

🪑

Real-Time Seat Maps

Live seat availability synced via WebSockets. See which seats are taken the moment someone books — no page refresh needed.

📲

QR Ticket Scanning

Every booking generates a unique QR ticket. Gate staff can scan and validate in seconds, even without an internet connection.

💳

Integrated Payments

Razorpay-powered payments for paid events. Automatic refunds on cancellation. Revenue split between your institution and the platform.

🏛️

Multi-Institution

Each college gets its own isolated space. Admins, events, and bookings are fully tenant-scoped with zero data bleed between institutions.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Track occupancy rates, revenue trends, popular events, and cluster-level attendance — all with exportable CSV reports.

🔔

Smart Waitlist

When an event is full, students can join the waitlist. The moment a seat frees up, the next person in line is notified instantly.

How it works

Three steps. Done.

AuditoriaX is designed to be instant. No long forms, no email chains, no confusion.

🎓

Register your institution

Sign up in under 2 minutes. Your college gets its own isolated admin space with its auditoriums, events, and members.

📅

Create & publish events

Add your auditoriums, set seat capacity, configure pricing, and publish events. Students can see them instantly.

🎟️

Book, pay & attend

Students pick their seat, pay if needed, and get a QR ticket in their inbox. Gate staff scan it. Done.
